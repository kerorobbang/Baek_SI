About Me

I am a remote sensing researcher at Pusan National University working across optical remote sensing platforms — unmanned aerial vehicles, manned aircraft, and satellite sensors. My work focuses on extracting quantitative environmental information from multispectral and hyperspectral imagery.

Recent projects span red tide and ocean-color quantification, hyperspectral mapping of tidal flats and coastal vegetation, radiometric and geometric calibration of drone-mounted cameras, and machine-learning approaches to problems such as forest-fire classification and material property estimation. I care about turning raw imagery into reliable, reproducible measurements.
